## Demonstrations and contact behavior

Synthetic records use reserved documentation addresses and example hostnames. Unrelated live third-party hosts and revenue/transaction examples were removed from the visible demonstrations. Service details, banners and vulnerability context are consistent. The API illustration uses explicit endpoint/authentication placeholders, shows host-search data and states that the example is illustrative. Its request/response layout reflows within its frame; small text is not reduced by scaling the entire panel. The checked GET, secondary-text and code-syntax color roles have contrast ratios of 5.02, 5.78 and 4.67 respectively.

Contact drafts use per-tab session storage with a 24-hour lifetime and explicit clearing. A memory fallback preserves operation when browser storage is unavailable. Preparing an email is not presented as successful server delivery. Users can copy the prepared text or select it in a read-only preview when email or clipboard handling is unavailable. All three form actions have at least a 44 × 44 px target and are checked for containment and non-overlap. The final target-size check caught a 42 px Clear draft button; source was corrected without relaxing the assertion.

### Resolution of initial failures

The six Chromium failures were not suppressed or skipped. Two assertions required mobile related-page buttons to remain right-aligned; the updated contract positively requires the intended left rail and keeps desktop/legal/footer checks. One expected an invalid Contact query parameter to erase a saved topic; its replacement checks both draft preservation and an empty field after explicit clearing. Three assumed Contact had one right-aligned submit action; the replacement checks the three actual actions for left alignment, visibility, target size, containment and non-overlap.

Focused checks then exposed two genuine remaining defects: the intermediate Teams metric grid did not follow structural rails, and Clear draft was 42 px high. Both were fixed in source. Teams passed all ten widths after the mobile rows/equal desktop columns correction. The form target was raised to 44 px. Firefox word breaking was also corrected in source and passed unchanged localized layout assertions. Earlier failure results were retained in the review workspace rather than represented as successful runs.

### Visual coverage and limits

The matrix covers 19 English pages, 11 Russian pages, 11 Traditional Chinese pages and a localized 404 state for each language. Its widths are 320, 390, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1856 px. Capturing a state is not a substitute for inspecting it.

Final manual review covered header, hero, lower actions and footer in all 176 desktop/mobile theme captures. Detailed review also covered changed demo cards, API, Closing, localized Pricing and Contact controls. Traditional Chinese Monitoring, API, Contact and Pricing were inspected from top to bottom in both mobile themes. Runtime tests separately cover menus, validation, period changes, monthly/annual prices, playback, service selection and other dynamic states. This is not a fresh manual inspection of every pixel in the middle of every long page or every possible animation frame.

Local width/color flags were investigated. Footer flags came from expanded hit-area pseudo-elements; transparent button ancestors did not describe the actual filled label surface. At Home 320 px, the long counter extends 5.5 px beyond its internal track but retains 10.5 px before the clipping panel edge, with every digit visible. At Responsible Scanning 320/390 px, Destination exceeds its nominal track but has a 6 px gap before the next text. These two small internal geometry limits remain, without visible clipping, overlap or document scroll in those states. A blank related-artwork slot in an early Teams capture was a lazy-image timing artifact; subsequent captures await image decoding.

Automated scans do not establish complete WCAG conformance. Container browser checks do not establish physical iOS/Android device approval or verify the authenticated product. The local Firefox/WebKit run covers the core and new regression suites above, not the complete 566-case contract in each engine; the full three-engine CI workflow remains available for that broader gate.
